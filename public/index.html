About Auction XI

What is Auction XI?

Auction XI is a football auction draft application that simulates a one-time, competitive squad-building experience. Build your dream eleven by bidding against friends in a dynamic, strategic auction where every decision matters.

How it Works

Every participant starts with the same budget. Players are presented one by one based on the selected formation, and participants bid to acquire them for their squad. When the timer runs out, the highest bid signs the player. Once everyone's formation is full, the squads are compared - and can be played off against each other in a match simulation.

Game Modes

Auction XI has two modes, and they play very differently:

  • Classic 11 v 11 - a full eleven across 5 real formations, on a 1,000M budget with 5M bid steps. Players are priced by rating, and the best come out first: quality steps down phase by phase. Each slot is its own signing, so the left and right centre back are separate players (shown as LCB and RCB).
  • 6 v 6 Futsal - six a side on a 100M budget with 1M bid steps. Every player opens at 1M regardless of who they are, and the order is completely random, so a superstar can appear in the final round. Slots are interchangeable: any forward fills either forward spot. Matches are simulated as futsal, which means far more goals.

Key Features

  • Real player database with 1,500+ footballers from top leagues and legends
  • Curated player pools (all-time, World Cup 2026 and more) selectable in every mode
  • Player nationality shown on every player offered
  • Two game modes: Classic 11 v 11 and 6 v 6 Futsal, each with their own formations and economy
  • 5 tactical formations in Classic (4-3-3, 4-2-3-1, 4-4-2, 3-4-3, 5-3-2)
  • Configurable auction timer (10s to unlimited)
  • Optional reroll system - a limited pool for the whole draft, voted on by the room online
  • Dynamic player progression for balanced drafts
  • Offline mode for sharing one screen with friends
  • Online multiplayer mode with real-time synced rooms
  • AI opponents mode for solo play
  • Live squad visualization on realistic football pitch
  • Budget management with position-based restrictions
  • Pause/resume functionality
  • Comprehensive draft statistics
  • Export results as JSON or shareable text

Privacy Policy

Local and AI play

Player data, fonts, and application assets are served by this application. Preferences and tutorial progress stay in browser storage. No analytics or advertising trackers are loaded.

Multiplayer rooms

Room codes, participant names, chat, squads, bids, and match results are shared with this application's own room server so connected devices can play together. Room snapshots are stored on the local server and expire after 24 hours without activity by default.

Clearing data

Clear this site's browser storage to reset preferences. Stop the server and remove its data directory to clear stored rooms. For questions about this local build, consult the project README.

About this local build

This is a separately hosted local recreation of the Auction XI application, prepared for independent development. It is not operated by the original Auction XI service.

Application and player data

The visual assets, game behavior, and player records were obtained from the publicly delivered application at auctionxi.app. Original source attribution and retrieval details are recorded in the project documentation. No connection to the original service's multiplayer database is used.

Development

The repository contains the application, the independent room server, tests, and documentation. Application hosting is configured separately.
